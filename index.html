Prevention

🌴 San Pablo, Keep It Clean—Fight Dengue as One! 🌴
Join your barangay's clean-up drive every Friday.
Let's make San Pablo a dengue-free city—remove standing water, cover containers, and report mosquito breeding sites to your local health office!
For updates, follow the San Pablo City Health Office Facebook.
Note: The following tips are based on recommendations from the WHO, CDC, and DOH Philippines.

Top 5 Dengue Prevention Tips

  1. Search and destroy mosquito breeding sites—empty, clean, or cover water containers weekly.
  2. Self-protection—use mosquito repellent, wear long sleeves and pants, and use screens or nets.
  3. Seek early consultation—visit your health center if you have fever for 2+ days, especially with rashes or pain behind the eyes.
  4. Support fogging/spraying in outbreak areas as advised by health authorities.
  5. Join community clean-up drives—a dengue-free San Pablo starts with you!

More Prevention Tips for San Pablo Residents

  • Check roof gutters weekly: Remove leaves and debris to prevent water collection.
  • Change water in vases and pet bowls every 2 days.
  • Cover water storage containers tightly.
  • Dispose of old tires, bottles, and cans properly.
  • Clean and scrub flower pots and trays regularly.
  • Install window and door screens.
  • Use mosquito coils or electric vapor mats as needed.
  • Encourage neighbors to join clean-up drives.
  • Report uncollected garbage or clogged drains to your barangay or the San Pablo City Health Office.
  • Educate children about dengue prevention.
Sources: World Health Organization (WHO) | Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) | Department of Health (Philippines)

About the System

This Dengue Monitoring System is a comprehensive tool designed to track, analyze, and predict dengue cases in San Pablo, Laguna. It provides real-time data, historical analysis, and predictive insights to help the local health unit manage and prevent outbreaks.

Features:

  • Dashboard: An at-a-glance overview of current cases, high-risk zones, and trends in San Pablo.
  • Predictions: Forecasts future dengue cases for San Pablo based on historical data.
  • Barangays: Detailed statistics for each barangay in San Pablo.
  • Yearly Analysis: In-depth charts showing trends over the years for San Pablo.
